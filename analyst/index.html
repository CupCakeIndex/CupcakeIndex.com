CUPCAKE_ANALYST
Your private stat desk. Paste a take, get the receipts.

How it works

  1. Paste a post or add a screenshot of it.
  2. Claude looks up only our numbers: weekly stats (NFL 2022 on, college 2024 on), our rankings, schedules and cupcake data. No numbers from memory.
  3. You get a verdict, the receipts, what the take leaves out, a chart, and two reply drafts to copy.

Runs on your Claude plan, so each check uses some of your usage. Nothing posts by itself.
