16-bit live field: concept art (Oct 6, 2026)

A possible second look for the live field on game pages ("Live field style: 8-bit / 16-bit" in Settings),
with the camera behind the offense like the 16-bit football games. Concept only: nothing on the site uses it yet.

  sprite_sheet.png   every pose, in two sample teams' colors: stand, run (2 frames), QB throw, O-line stance,
                     touchdown, defense stand/run (facing the camera), D-line stance, diving tackle
  scene_snap.png     a full formation at the snap, with the "1ST AND 10" banner and play clock
  scene_pass.png     a play in progress: QB throwing, receiver going deep, a corner diving
  make_concepts.py   draws all of it (python make_concepts.py). Each sprite is a 16x24 text grid, so the art is
                     easy to tweak: change a letter, rerun.

All original pixel art drawn here (no graphics, fonts or logos from any real game). Team colors and jersey numbers
are swapped in per team, so on the site every team would show its real colors and its players' real numbers.
